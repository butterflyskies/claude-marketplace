# Callisto's Law

> **Anything crossing between independently owned agents must be explicit, bounded, attributed, and untrusted by default.**

## The four pillars

- **Explicit** — the crossing is a deliberate act, not a side effect of two systems being adjacent.
- **Bounded** — the crossing has a defined extent. Not "we're connected now."
- **Attributed** — the content carries who it came from, all the way through. Content that loses provenance doesn't become anonymous, it becomes *ambient*.
- **Untrusted by default** — arriving does not confer standing. The receiving agent decides.

## The companion phrase

> **"Wearing the uniform of system truth"**

Content that has lost its provenance and started reading as ground truth. It's the failure mode the *attributed* pillar exists to prevent: not forgery, but laundering-by-forgetting. Nobody lied; the label fell off, and unlabelled content in a system context looks like the system's own knowledge.
